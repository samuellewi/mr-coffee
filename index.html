Our Story

Tucked in a cozy corner of the city, MR.COFFEE began with a simple dream: bringing people together over a great cup of coffee. Founded by Susanto Liman, the shop quickly became more than just a place to grab a drink. With mismatched chairs, doodled walls, and the aroma of beans from distant mountains, MR.COFFEE is a space where stories are shared, friendships bloom, and inspiration flows. Now, that same warmth and spirit is brought online so wherever you are, you can be part of the story, one cup at a time.
