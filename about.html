Susanto Liman
Founder & CEO of MR.COFFEE

Behind the rich aroma of coffee and the welcoming ambiance of MR.COFFEE is a visionary named Susanto Liman. With a background in coffee crafting and a passion for entrepreneurship, Susanto began his journey with just a small coffee corner in the heart of the city. Inspired by the magic of meaningful moments shared over a cup of coffee, he set out to build more than just a coffee shop—he dreamed of creating a space where stories are born and friendships are brewed.
His dedication to quality, creativity, and customer experience quickly turned MR.COFFEE into a brand that resonates with coffee lovers everywhere. Susanto believes that every cup tells a story, and every customer deserves to be part of that journey. Today, MR.COFFEE is a place where passion meets purpose—one cup at a time.

Our Mission

At MR.COFFEE, our mission is simple: to make coffee a delightful part of everyone’s everyday life. Whether you're a student in need of a morning boost, a professional taking a midday break, or a couple enjoying a cozy moment together—we believe great coffee should be accessible, affordable, and exceptional.
We are committed to sourcing high-quality beans, mastering brewing techniques, and offering a diverse menu to suit every palate. Through warm service, consistent quality, and a sense of community, we strive to turn your daily cup into something extraordinary.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the most recognized coffee brand that brings people together through shared moments, exceptional taste, and a sense of belonging. We aim to create a space—both online and offline—where stories, ideas, and laughter are exchanged freely.
By continuously innovating, empowering local communities, and supporting sustainability, MR.COFFEE envisions a future where each café is not just a business, but a part of the neighborhood—a place where culture, creativity, and connection flourish with every cup.

Our Journey

MR.COFFEE began in 2015 as a small café tucked in a quiet corner of the city, built on the simple dream of bringing people together over coffee. The idea was modest, but the impact was heartfelt—from the mismatched chairs and hand-drawn wall art to the intoxicating aroma of freshly brewed beans.
What started as one shop quickly became a gathering spot for friends, families, artists, and entrepreneurs. As word spread and the community grew, so did the vision. Today, MR.COFFEE continues to serve with the same passion and warmth, bringing its signature charm to more neighborhoods and now, to digital platforms as well. Wherever you are, you're invited to be a part of the story—because every story starts with a cup.
